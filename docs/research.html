Initiation of Coverage Utilities / Renewable

NextEra Energy — Stable earnings, priced for its quality

We initiate coverage of NextEra Energy with a Neutral rating and a sum-of-the-parts target price of $83.17. NextEra is one of the highest-quality utility franchises in North America — a top-tier regulated business, an industry-leading renewables platform, and favorable long-term demand trends. We believe the current share price already reflects much of that strength, leaving limited risk-adjusted upside from here.

Investment thesis
01

FPL anchors earnings growth

Florida Power & Light's constructive regulatory framework and rate-base expansion provide durable earnings and cash-flow growth, supporting the majority of consolidated profitability.

02

NEER drives long-term growth

A development backlog exceeding 30 GW gives NextEra Energy Resources clear visibility into future renewable capacity additions — the company's primary engine for revenue growth and long-term value creation.

03

Valuation reflects fundamental strength

The premium multiple is justified by NextEra's growth profile and renewable leadership — but current pricing already incorporates most of the expected benefit, limiting upside under our base case.

Key catalysts
  • Fed easing lowers financing costs and improves renewable project economics.
  • AI-driven electricity demand could accelerate procurement and PPA activity.
Key risks
  • Higher-for-longer rates compress project returns and slow deployment.
  • Interconnection delays could postpone backlog monetization.
