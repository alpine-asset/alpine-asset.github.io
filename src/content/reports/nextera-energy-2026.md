NextEra Energy screens as a best-in-class operator whose quality is well
understood by the market. Our Neutral rating is a statement about **price, not
franchise** — we would look to become more constructive on a pullback or on
evidence that the NEER backlog is converting to earnings faster than our base
case assumes.

## Where we could be wrong

The single most important swing factor is the pace of renewable deployment. If
AI-driven load growth pulls forward power-purchase agreements and the
interconnection queue clears faster than expected, NEER's backlog could
monetize ahead of schedule — a scenario that would support a rating upgrade.

Conversely, a higher-for-longer rate environment remains the key downside risk
to project returns and to the premium multiple the shares currently command.
